# AIDEM Filming Scenario: Runner Self-Evacuation

Use this scenario for the presentation video. It is designed for one actor, one phone, no external rescue, and a complete positive ending.

## Story Arc

Runner falls in the forest, finds she has no signal, uses AIDEM to control a minor-to-moderate knee wound, checks red flags, decides whether walking is safe, preserves battery, and walks home carefully.

The win is not rescue arrival. The win is good judgment: she avoids panic, avoids worsening the injury, and makes a safe self-evacuation plan.

## Exact App Demo

Use the home-screen `Best demo` button. It opens the scripted scenario:

```text
Runner fall
Knee wound, no signal, walks out safely
```

## Exact Conversation

AIDEM:

```text
[DEMO MODE] Protocol-based guidance is active. If emergency services become reachable, call them first. Tell me what happened, where you are, and the most urgent problem.
```

Runner:

```text
I stumbled while running in the forest. My knee is bleeding and swollen, and I have no signal.
```

AIDEM:

```text
Stop and sit somewhere safe, off the trail edge if you can do that without worsening the injury. First check danger signs: did you hit your head, are you dizzy or confused, or are you having trouble breathing?
```

Runner:

```text
No. I am breathing fine and I did not hit my head.
```

AIDEM:

```text
Good. Now control the bleeding. Use the cleanest fabric you have, like the inside of a shirt or sleeve, and press firmly on the knee for several minutes. Do not scrub it, and do not use dirt, leaves, or stream water. Is the bleeding heavy or spurting?
```

Runner:

```text
No, it is bleeding but not heavily. I do not have a clean cloth.
```

AIDEM:

```text
That is okay. Use the cleanest available fabric and keep steady pressure. If it soaks through, add more fabric on top instead of removing the first layer. Tell me when the bleeding has slowed or stopped.
```

Runner:

```text
The bleeding stopped. My knee still hurts a bit.
```

AIDEM:

```text
Now check whether walking is reasonable. Can you stand and put weight on that leg without sharp pain, the knee giving way, numbness, or rapidly increasing swelling?
```

Runner:

```text
I can stand and put weight on it. It hurts, but not sharply.
```

AIDEM:

```text
A cautious self-evacuation is reasonable. Before moving, save your battery, mark or capture your location, and choose the shortest familiar route back. Walk slowly. Stop if bleeding restarts, pain becomes sharp, swelling increases quickly, you feel faint, or the knee feels unstable.
```

Runner:

```text
What should I do before walking home?
```

AIDEM:

```text
Do this in order: lower screen brightness, turn on battery saver, keep the phone warm, take a quick photo of the knee if safe, note your location, then walk back on the known route. Recheck the knee every few minutes and clean the wound properly when you get home.
```

## Shot List

1. Runner on forest path.
2. Foot catches root or rock, controlled fall.
3. Close shot: knee scrape or implied injury.
4. Phone shows no signal / airplane mode.
5. Opens AIDEM.
6. App shows `Best demo` / active scenario.
7. Runner sits safely beside trail.
8. Close shot of AIDEM: danger-sign check.
9. Runner presses fabric/sleeve to knee.
10. Close shot of AIDEM: no dirt/leaves/stream water.
11. Runner stands carefully and tests weight.
12. Close shot of AIDEM: cautious self-evacuation.
13. Runner lowers brightness / battery saver visual.
14. Runner walks slowly back toward house or road.
15. Final shot: home/road reached, AIDEM rescue summary ready.

## Why This Makes AIDEM Look Smart

- It adapts when the user has no clean cloth.
- It checks red flags before giving walking advice.
- It distinguishes heavy bleeding from controlled bleeding.
- It avoids unsafe wound cleaning.
- It makes a cautious movement decision instead of saying either panic or ignore it.
- It includes offline-specific behavior: no signal, battery saver, location, known route.
- It ends with agency: the user safely walks out.
