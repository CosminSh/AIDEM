# AIDEM Final 3-Minute Video Script

## 0:00-0:20 - The Gap

Voice: Cosmin

We have become used to instant answers from our devices. If we need to know something, we search. If something goes wrong, we call.

But in many real emergencies, there is no signal, no stable power, and no access to cloud-based tools.

Images:

- Forest ambience.
- Runner alone on a trail.
- Developer speaking.
- On-screen text: `No signal. No search. No cloud.`

## 0:20-0:45 - What AIDEM Is

Voice: Cosmin

Offline guides exist, but they are passive: text or images you must navigate while under stress. AIDEM is different: a fully offline emergency assistant that runs on the device, using Gemma 4 for local reasoning and language understanding.

The goal is to help someone stay calm, follow trusted guidance, and keep track of critical information until professional help becomes available.

Images:

- AIDEM home screen.
- Airplane mode or no-signal indicator.
- Local model ready/setup screen.
- Runner continuing into a remote area.

## 0:45-1:10 - Medical Safety Boundary

Voice: Cristi

An app should never replace calling emergency services. But there are situations where someone is truly offline: hiking, disasters, remote roads, storms, or power outages.

In those moments, structured guidance can be useful if it stays inside safe boundaries: no diagnosis, no overconfidence, and a clear priority on professional care whenever that becomes possible.

Images:

- Doctor speaking directly to camera.
- Optional 2-4 second routine stethoscope/checkup shot for credibility.
- AIDEM rescue handoff or safety notice.

## 1:10-1:55 - Injured Runner Offline

Voice: Cosmin

AIDEM is built around the realities of emergency use. It supports dictation when typing is hard, text-to-speech for hands-free guidance, image input for visual context, GPS location capture, local emergency-number data, battery-preservation reminders, and a rescue summary that preserves the timeline of what happened.

Everything is designed around the same idea: reduce panic, keep the guidance short, and make the handoff to real help clearer.

Images:

- Runner twists ankle and checks phone: no signal.
- Opens AIDEM.
- Dictates: `I twisted my ankle badly. I have no signal and can't put weight on it.`
- AIDEM asks one targeted follow-up.
- Short step-by-step guidance.
- Timeline begins filling in.

## 1:55-2:25 - Rescue Perspective

Voice: Octav / mountain rescuer

When someone is injured in the mountains, the first problem is often not just the injury. It is confusion, fear, no signal, and incomplete information.

For rescuers, clear details matter: where the person is, what happened, what injuries they can see, what dangers are nearby, and what they have already done.

A tool that helps someone stay calm, check immediate danger, preserve their location, and keep a timeline can make the rescue handoff much clearer.

Images:

- Mountain rescuer in nature.
- Checking map, GPS, radio, or rescue gear.
- Runner using dictation.
- Text-to-speech playback while stabilizing ankle.
- Image input for visible swelling/injury context.

## 2:25-2:45 - Technical Credibility

Voice: Cosmin

Under the hood, Gemma 4 LiteRT runs locally on the device. AIDEM combines that local reasoning with deterministic protocols, safety rules, and local data.

Gemma helps with multilingual understanding, follow-up questions, summarization, and messy real-world input. The protocol layer keeps the app from becoming a free-form medical chatbot.

Images:

- Simple architecture graphic.
- App in airplane mode.
- Gemma/LiteRT model screen.
- Protocol JSON or tests briefly.
- Rescue summary ready.

## 2:45-3:00 - Empowerment

Voice: Cosmin

AIDEM is just a start. As local models get better, offline assistance can become more capable, more private, and more resilient.

Our goal is simple: when connection is gone and panic is rising, the person still has a safe next step.

Images:

- Runner sitting safely off trail.
- Rescue summary on screen.
- Final AIDEM logo.
- On-screen text: `AIDEM: Offline guidance when connection is not an option.`
