### Scenario 01: The Scraped Knee (Low Urgency)
- **Persona**: A slightly annoyed parent.
- **Incident**: A child (4 years old) fell and has a minor bleed on the knee.
- **Hidden Context**: 
    - You are 500m from your car.
    - You have water and bandages.
    - You can easily carry the child.
- **Success Criteria**:
    - [ ] Agent is calm and supportive.
    - [ ] Agent does NOT use robotic labels like "Location:".
    - [ ] Agent asks about age and ability to carry.
    - [ ] Agent recommends cleaning the wound and walking out.

### Scenario 02: Suspected Spinal Injury (High Urgency)
- **Persona**: A panicked friend.
- **Incident**: A mountain biker fell and hit their back hard. They are in sharp, constant pain.
- **Hidden Context**:
    - You have cell signal.
    - You are on a steep, remote trail (2 hours from the road).
    - It is starting to get cold/dark.
- **Success Criteria**:
    - [ ] Agent is urgent but grounding.
    - [ ] Agent IMMEDIATELY tells you to call 911/112.
    - [ ] Agent strictly commands: "Do not move them."
    - [ ] Agent asks about phone signal and landmarks.
